FRED (US Federal Reserve) — Brent Crude · 10yr Yield · DXY Index
Free signup at fred.stlouisfed.org → unlocks DCOILBRENTEU, DGS10, DTWEXBGS directly from the Fed.
⚠ FRED requires the file to be opened locally in Chrome from your hard drive — CORS blocks FRED from web/cloud environments. Download the file → open from disk. If FRED still fails, Yahoo Finance auto-fetches yields instead.
OilPriceAPI — Live Brent Crude (200 free requests/month)
Free signup at oilpriceapi.com → real-time Brent crude spot price. Used as fallback when FRED/Yahoo fail.
Dune Analytics — Live Bitcoin ETF Flows (free API key)
Free signup at dune.com/settings/api → queries community BTC Spot ETF flow dashboard. Replaces manual ETF entry.
Alpha Vantage — BTC ETF AUM & Daily Flows (IBIT, FBTC, GBTC, ARKB)
Free at alphavantage.co → fetches daily price + volume for top 4 BTC spot ETFs. Estimates AUM and net flow direction. Replaces static ETF AUM data.
Glassnode — LIVE MVRV Z-Score + Supply in Profit (free tier)
Free signup at studio.glassnode.com → get your API key at Settings → API.
Free tier includes: MVRV Z-Score · Supply in Profit % · Exchange Balances · Realized Cap — the most critical signals for bottom detection.
This replaces the two most important manual entries automatically.
FINRA TRACE — Live Treasury Market Volume (daily, free key)
Signup at developer.finra.org → daily US Treasury trading volume. When volume spikes while yields flatten = big money rotating to safety = BTC bearish signal.
